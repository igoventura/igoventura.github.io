~/about

I started professionally in 2012 in Fortaleza, writing software for healthcare and education companies, and I've been on the full-stack side of things ever since. Intern to tech lead to senior engineer, which mostly means I've made every category of mistake at least once and have opinions about avoiding them.

My core stack is .NET and Kotlin on the backend with Angular and React on the front, and I've been pulling Go into anything that needs to run for a long time without me. I've built Web APIs at scale, shipped native Android clients, and led small teams through rewrites.

Lately a large part of my workflow is agentic — Claude Code and its subagents do a real share of my scaffolding, test writing and migration legwork. I keep the merge button where I can see it.

Off the clock: cooking, dancing and videogames — none of it compiles, which is most of the appeal.
