~/about

I started professionally in 2012 in Fortaleza, writing software for healthcare and education companies, and I've been on the full-stack side of things ever since. I went from intern to tech lead to senior engineer, which mostly means I've made every category of mistake at least once and have opinions about avoiding them.

My core stack is .NET Core and Kotlin on the backend with React and Node.js on the front. I've built and maintained Web APIs at scale, shipped Android clients, and led small teams through rewrites. Lately a large part of my workflow is agentic — Claude Code and its subagents do a real share of my scaffolding, test writing and migration legwork.

Based in São Paulo. I've worked with teams in Brazil, the US and fully remote, so I'm comfortable being the asynchronous one on the call.
